## Description

TrustPay is an escrow-powered Mini App that enables secure buyer-seller transactions. It protects both parties by holding funds until the buyer confirms successful delivery of goods or services, making online shopping and marketplace deals safer and more trustworthy.

## Builder story

TrustPay was inspired by a common problem in online transactions: buyers fear paying first, while sellers fear delivering without getting paid. I wanted to remove that uncertainty by creating a decentralized escrow solution that protects both parties. Built on the Nimiq ecosystem, TrustPay makes digital transactions simple, secure, and based on transparency instead of blind trust.
