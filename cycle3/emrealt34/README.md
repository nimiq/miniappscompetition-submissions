## Description

Nimshop is a gift-card and digital-goods storefront built for Nimiq Pay that uses the blockchain three ways: pay with NIM and earn cashback, stake with our Nimiq Base Staking pool (80M NIM) to boost it by up to +10%, and rate and review every order on-chain.

## Builder story

At the start of the second cycle, we asked the Nimiq team to add Bitcoin Lightning support for mini apps. Many projects have since started using it, and thanks to our request, Lightning payments are now possible in mini apps. We’re proud that Nimshop helped push this forward.

Nimshop never holds a customer balance, so there is nothing to withdraw and nothing to lose.

Stake. We developed Nimiq Base Staking specifically to pay these cashbacks, and it has reached 80 million NIM at the time of writing. Stake with it and your cashback rate rises from the base rate up to +10%, with a loyalty ramp that rewards people who stay.

Review. Every order can be rated from 1 to 5 and commented on by sending a transaction to the blockchain, so reviews are public and permanent.

Gift emails are customized for Nimiq too. When you send a gift card to someone outside the ecosystem, the email introduces them to Nimiq and gets them started.
